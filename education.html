The Big Divide

Temporary vs. Permanent — the difference in one breath.

All life insurance answers one question: who gets what, and for how long? Temporary answers it for a period. Permanent answers it for life — and quietly builds wealth along the way.

Temporary

Term Life Insurance

You pay a low premium for a set number of years. If you pass during the term, your beneficiaries receive the death benefit. It's pure protection — the most efficient way to cover your income-earning years and the people who rely on them.

Best for:

  • Young families protecting a mortgage and income
  • Covering debt and kids' years at home
  • Maximum coverage at the lowest cost
Permanent

Whole & Universal Life

Coverage that never expires, with a cash-value account that grows tax-deferred. You can borrow against it, use it for retirement, or leave a guaranteed legacy. It costs more — but it becomes a lifelong asset, not just a expense.

Best for:

  • Lifelong coverage and final expenses
  • Building tax-advantaged cash value (IUL)
  • Legacy, estate, and business planning
