The Two Families of Coverage

Temporary or permanent? Both protect. They just work differently.

The first question in any life insurance decision is simple: do you need coverage for a period of time, or for life? Here's the starting point.

01 — Temporary

Term Life Insurance

Coverage for a set number of years (10, 20, 30). It's the most affordable way to protect the people who depend on your income during your peak-earning decades. If the term ends and you're still healthy, you simply walk away — no cash value, no strings.

How term applies to you →
02 — Permanent

Whole & Universal Life

Coverage that lasts your entire life and builds cash value you can borrow against or use later. It costs more, but it never expires and can become a quiet financial engine — especially through Indexed Universal Life (IUL).

The Income Side

Annuities: turning savings into a paycheck you can't outlive.

Insurance also works in reverse. An annuity is a contract that converts a lump sum or a stream of premiums into guaranteed income — often for life. It's how many retirees make sure the money lasts as long as they do.

  • Fixed annuities — predictable, guaranteed rates
  • Fixed Indexed annuities — upside potential with a floor
  • Lifetime income riders — income you cannot outlive
  • Used for retirement, legacy, and long-term care planning
